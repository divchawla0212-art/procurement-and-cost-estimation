# Bid list — containerized gas engine generator sets

**Everything below is invented.** The three companies do not exist. Their
turnover bands, ratings, award histories and principals were written for a
demonstration of this platform and are not a record of anybody's capability.
They are on this page because the round they are used in — the clarification
exchange against `ADN-AEC-ME-SPC-026 Rev 0` — is built on a real client
material requisition, and putting a real packager's name against questions
nobody asked would be indistinguishable, on screen, from a record of what they
did ask. So the document is real and the bidders are not, which is the same
line `workflow/avl_import.py` draws for the vendor registry.

The ADNOC references are to a genuine material requisition and a genuine
approved vendor list. Nothing here is a communication from, to, or on behalf
of that organisation.

**None of the three is on ADNOC's approved vendor list, and none of them says
it is.** An earlier draft of this page marked two of them approved by ADNOC to
give the shortlist an approved row to sit beside an unapproved one. That was a
fabricated client approval — a company ADNOC has never heard of, shown on
screen exactly as a company on their list would be — and it is the one thing
this platform's rules are least willing to tolerate, because the whole value of
the column is that it is read rather than asserted. What these three carry is
Astra qualification, which is invented in the same direction as the companies
themselves and claims nothing about the client.

The approved rows on the shortlist come from somewhere else, and they are real:
[three vendors genuinely registered for `GENERATOR POWER-OTHERS`](#the-three-approved-vendors-invited-alongside)
in the December 2025 export. They receive the enquiry and they ask nothing —
being invited is a fact this mock may make up about a real company on a
development store, and asking a question is not.

---

## The package

Two containerized gas engine driven generator sets, 525 kW, 415 V 3-phase
4-wire 50 Hz, duty S1, tags G-4101-01A and G-4101-01B — one running, one
standby, feeding the P5 compression facility at Habshan. Vendor scope runs from
the alternator through the acoustic enclosure, starting and lube oil systems,
F&G and CO₂ fire protection, to the LCP in the container and the GCP in the
substation. Estimated 18.4 m AED, required on site 30 November 2025.

Scoped to **`GENERATOR POWER-OTHERS`**, and that is not a shrug. The approved
vendor list's controlled vocabulary has diesel-, gas-turbine- and
steam-turbine-driven generators and no gas-engine-driven group at all. A
reciprocating gas genset is not a gas turbine, so it falls to the catch-all —
worth knowing before anyone reads a nine-vendor candidate list as a nine-vendor
market.

## The three that ask the questions

Invented, Astra-qualified, and deliberately not three of the same company. Each
brings a different thing to a package that is really three packages in a box —
an engine, an alternator, and a container with cooling and acoustics around
them — and each one's clarification questions come from the part it owns. That
is the useful test of a bid list: if all three ask the same question, two of
them are padding.

### Falcon Bay Rotating Equipment · Mussafah, UAE

Rotating-equipment packager with its own alternator and switchgear integration
shop. The only bidder of the three that can be held to the insulation and
temperature-rise clauses without going back to a principal for every answer,
which matters on a specification whose items 2.6 and 2.7 contradict each other
on exactly that point.

| | |
|---|---|
| Approvals | **Astra only — not on the ADNOC list** |
| Prequalification | Approved, expires 31 Mar 2027 |
| Turnover band | AED 250–500 m |
| Performance rating | 4.2 |
| Past awards | 6 |
| Represents | Waukesha, Marelli Motori |

Six awards on ADNOC onshore packages, **none containerized at this rating** —
the gap on this list worth having in front of you. Reads specifications
closely, which is a virtue at Clarifications and a nuisance at bid evaluation.

Raised **TQ-001** (Class F/Class B against Class H — the two cannot both
govern, and at the 58 °C site ambient they give different frame sizes) and
**CQ-001** (item 2.47 is headed "Gas Pressure Regulating Skid" but its
requirement text is item 2.46's, verbatim, so the skid's actual duty is
undefined).

### Jebel Ali Power Systems · Jebel Ali, UAE

The gas-engine specialist, and the only one of the three with sour-service
running hours behind it. Largest by turnover, best rated, most awarded.

| | |
|---|---|
| Approvals | **Astra only — not on the ADNOC list** |
| Prequalification | Approved, **expires 30 Nov 2026** |
| Turnover band | AED 500 m – 1 bn |
| Performance rating | 4.5 |
| Past awards | 11 |
| Represents | Jenbacher, Stamford |

Two things to carry into evaluation. Its prequalification has **the shortest
runway on the list** — an award that slips past November 2026 needs it renewed
first, and the platform derives expiry against the date you ask on rather than
storing a status that would quietly go stale. And its alternator comes from a
single principal, which is not a preference but a constraint, and it is the
reason for its sub-vendor question.

Raised **TQ-002** (at 700 ppm H₂S the requested oil-change interval sits well
below a standard service interval, and the engine builder's warranty normally
depends on fuel treatment — is any provided upstream of the battery limit?) and
**TQ-003** (its standard alternator is not on the Attachment-5 AVL, though it
is approved on two other assets: concession, or repackage and reprice?).

### Northgate Energy Packages · United Kingdom

Containerization and acoustics house. Enclosure design, cooling and emissions
treatment are the work it does itself rather than buys, which is why the noise
limit and the SCR pricing are the two things it asks about.

| | |
|---|---|
| Approvals | Astra only — not on the ADNOC list |
| Prequalification | Approved, expires 30 Jun 2028 |
| Turnover band | AED 100–250 m |
| Performance rating | 3.9 |
| Past awards | 2 |
| Represents | MWM |

Smallest by turnover and by awards. Like the other two it carries the
**client-approval caution** — procurement has qualified it, the client has not
been asked — which shows on its shortlist row as *Not on the ADNOC list*. That
is a caution and never a blocker: the vendor is invited, the bid is comparable,
and the gap is something to resolve before award rather than a reason to leave
a capable packager off the enquiry. The three rows below it on the same table
show what the other state looks like.

Raised **CQ-002** (item 2.51 prices SCR as an option while item 2.8.2 demands
Attachment-2 emission compliance — bids are not comparable until this is fixed
for everyone) and **TQ-004** (85 dB(A) at 1 m against a stated preference for
air cooling — is the limit to be met with the fans at full speed at 58 °C?).

## The three approved vendors, invited alongside

These are real companies, quoted from the `Vendor Name` column of the December
2025 export — truncation and all, because that column is cut at 35 characters
and lengthening a name to its full company style would stop it matching the
registry row it names. All three are genuinely registered for `GENERATOR
POWER-OTHERS` and are approved by both ADNOC and Astra, which is what puts a
vendor in front of a buyer here.

| Vendor | Principal on that product group |
|---|---|
| GENERAL NAVIGATION & COMMERCE COMPA | Detroit Diesel |
| OILTEK TRADING-SOLE PROPRIETORSHIPL | Sterling Generators |
| Ali & Sons Oil-Field Supplies & Ser | Intergen |

These three of the six the export lists, because the manufacturer recorded
against their `GENERATOR POWER-OTHERS` row builds generator sets. The other
three are registered for that group behind an alternator or a power-conversion
principal, which is a different offer against a containerized genset enquiry.
That is a judgement about the package, not about the companies.

**No profile is written for them anywhere** — not here, not in the fixture. What
the platform shows for them came out of the export. A turnover band or a rating
invented beside a real name would be the same error as the fabricated approval,
one field to the left.

**And they raise nothing.** A shortlist row records that somebody was asked to
bid. A query records what somebody said. The round these six questions are
modelled on actually took place, and its minutes sit two directories from the
requisition, so a real company shown asking a question it never asked is the
one confusion this whole arrangement exists to prevent.

## What it all adds up to

| | Falcon Bay | Jebel Ali | Northgate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Owns the alternator | ✅ | ❌ single principal | ❌ |
| Sour-gas hours | ❌ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Containerization in house | ❌ | partial | ✅ |
| On the client's list | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Prequalification runway | Mar 2027 | **Nov 2026** | Jun 2028 |

Six vendors on the shortlist, three of whom asked something. Six queries, four
of them internal contradictions in the requisition rather than vendor
preferences. Two — the insulation clause and the SCR pricing basis — change
what is being priced, so they have to be answered and circulated to the whole
shortlist before any bid is comparable. That is the round this bid list exists
to run, and it is open now: no answers, gate closed, and the closure reason
names all six.
